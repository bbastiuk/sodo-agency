6. The Meta pixel and browser storage

The site stores your chosen language and your pixel choice in browser storage (localStorage). This is needed for the site to work and does not require consent.

The Meta pixel is switched on only after you click “Accept”. Meta may then set cookies (e.g. _fbp) and receives information about visits and clicks on the site, which we use to measure our ads. Meta also processes this data under its own privacy policy.

You can withdraw consent at any time with the “Cookie settings” button at the bottom of the home page.

You have the right to access, rectify and erase your data, to restrict its processing and to data portability, and to object to processing based on our legitimate interest. You can withdraw consent at any time; this doesn't affect the lawfulness of processing before withdrawal. Just write to online@sodoagency.eu.

You can also lodge a complaint with the President of the Personal Data Protection Office in Poland (uodo.gov.pl).

8. Automated decisions
